FAQ

Questions answered.

Does Elnovar recruit HGV drivers?

HGV and transport roles are within our initial focus. Any assignment would require a confirmed brief and commercial agreement.

Can employers hire drivers permanently?

Permanent recruitment is the initial model. Temporary driver supply is a more complex arrangement and is not presented as operational yet.

Can Elnovar confirm driver compliance?

Role-specific licence and eligibility checks need an agreed process, appropriate permission and verification with relevant sources.
