PRIVACY & DATA HANDLING

Pre-launch privacy information.

This page describes the current preview website and the information required before live employer or candidate enquiries are accepted.

CURRENT WEBSITE STATUS

Enquiries are not yet activated.

Elnovar Group is establishing its recruitment offering. The website's enquiry form is a preview until the business has verified its legal identity, privacy notice, secure contact endpoint and operational policies. Please do not enter candidate CV details or other personal information in the preview form.

01

What the website does today

The website displays service information and a inactive enquiry form. While the form is inactive, it does not send or store the information typed into it. Hosting providers and external assets such as fonts or photographs may nonetheless process ordinary technical request information needed to deliver their services.

02

How a live enquiry would work

After verified setup, a separate protected server-side endpoint can receive limited enquiry details through HTTPS and deliver them to a monitored recipient mailbox. Anti-spam verification will be required. No candidate CV upload or recruitment database is enabled by this change.

03

Information required before launch

Before activating live collection, Elnovar Group must publish its verified legal controller name, contact details, purpose and lawful basis of processing, recipients and processors, retention periods, applicable international-transfer safeguards, individual rights and complaint information, including the appropriate UK ICO contact route.

04

Recruitment-specific safeguards

Candidate experience, contact details and CVs must not be collected or shared with employers before suitable notices, access controls, retention rules and lawful sharing arrangements are established. Automated matching or screening would require its own additional review and clear disclosures.

Privacy is part of operational readiness.

This page describes the current data-handling position, not a complete statutory privacy notice. The page must be completed and approved before the secure form is enabled.
