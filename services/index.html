What is available today?

Initial focus describes the recruitment services we're prioritising as we launch, not a claim about a pre-existing candidate database or established client delivery. By enquiry and specialist setup mean we'll assess feasibility and delivery arrangements before proposing a contract. In development means the capability is planned, not a live product or service.

Any recruitment fee, payment trigger, replacement arrangement or other service obligation must be agreed in writing before work proceeds. We do not charge work-seekers for finding them employment.
