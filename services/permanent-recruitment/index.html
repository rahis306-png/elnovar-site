01

A straightforward approach to permanent hiring

We start with a practical briefing on responsibilities, essential qualifications, salary, location, shift pattern and the hiring timetable. The aim is to find relevant people rather than simply forward CVs.

02

Where a placement fee fits

Permanent recruitment is typically agreed on a contingency or retained basis. Elnovar's intended starting model is employer-funded and success-based, with the exact percentage, payment event, ownership and replacement terms defined before any introduction.

03

Recruitment options to discuss

An employer may need a single permanent hire, a fixed-fee shortlist, an exclusive search or multiple appointments. The commercial arrangement should match the complexity and recruitment volume rather than follow a one-size-fits-all price.

04

Candidate screening and human judgment

Candidate suitability depends on evidence such as relevant experience, licences or qualifications, shift availability and location. Any technology-assisted screening should support, not replace, human assessment.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Do employers pay candidates' recruitment fees?

No. Elnovar's permanent recruitment model is employer-funded. Recruitment agencies generally cannot charge work-seekers for finding them employment.

What is the recruitment fee?

Fees are quoted for each brief and agreed in writing before candidate introductions. They may be percentage-based or fixed-fee depending on the assignment.

Which roles are in scope?

Our initial focus covers maintenance engineers, technicians, HGV and transport operations roles, warehousing supervisors and related operational positions.
